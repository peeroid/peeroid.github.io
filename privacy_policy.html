Privacy Policy

Peeroid Network Application

Last Updated: February 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Peeroid ("we", "our", or "the Application"). This document explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our peer-to-peer (P2P) networking services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a clear understanding of our data practices.

2. Google User Data

Our application, Peeroid, allows you to link your Google Account to enhance your experience with AI features and account personalization. When you choose to link your Google Account, we access specific information from Google.

What Google data do we collect?

  • Basic Profile Information: Including your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Gemini API Capabilities: Access to your Generative Language API quota and retrieval services to power AI-driven features within the application.

How do we use your Google data?

  • Authentication: To securely identify you and manage your linked account state.
  • Personalization: To display your name and profile picture within your local application instance.
  • AI Services (Gemini): To provide AI-powered features such as automated content generation, summarization, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) directly through your Google API quota.

Peeroid does not store your Google password and does not sell any data retrieved from Google APIs to third parties.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Peeroid's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Information Collected Automatically

As a P2P application, Peeroid operates primarily by establishing direct connections between devices. We collect minimal information automatically to facilitate these connections:

  • Connection Metadata: IP addresses and network identifiers required for peer discovery and DHT (Distributed Hash Table) operations.
  • Device Information: Operating system and browser type to optimize the application performance for your device.
  • Local Storage: We use local storage on your device to save your settings and application state. Most of your user-generated content resides only on your device or the devices you specifically share it with.

4. How We Share Your Information

Peeroid is built on a decentralization model:

  • Peer Privacy: Your data is shared directly with the peers you choose to communicate with. We do not act as an intermediary for your private messages.
  • Public Identifiers: Your Libp2p Peer ID and any public profile information you set are visible to other peers in the Network to facilitate discovery.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Peeroid is a client-side P2P application, you have direct control over your data. Most data can be deleted by clearing your browser's local storage or application database. You can un-link your Google Account at any time through the application settings to revoke our access to your Google data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
